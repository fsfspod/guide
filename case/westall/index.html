The Westall School Sighting

Catalog FG-037When April 6, 1966Where Melbourne, Australia

Hundreds of students and teachers watch an object descend near their school.

Students and staff at Westall High School and the neighboring primary school said a silvery object came down behind trees in a nearby field, then rose and left, possibly chased by small planes. Many witnesses still tell the same story decades later.

Weigh the evidence

The ledger

What the record shows, what people say, and the best explanation available so far.

What's documented

  • Local newspapers covered it at the time, and witnesses gathered for a 2016 reunion.

What's claimed

  • A craft landed and officials told students to keep quiet.

Best explanation so far

Researchers have proposed a balloon from a joint Australian–U.S. high-altitude research program that was launching from the area in 1966. It is a plausible fit but not proven.
