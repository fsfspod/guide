The Aztec Crash Hoax

Catalog FG-014When 1949–1952Where Aztec, New Mexico

A bestselling book about a crashed saucer, built on a con man's sales pitch.

Columnist Frank Scully's 1950 bestseller described a saucer that crashed near Aztec with 16 small bodies aboard. His sources, Silas Newton and a man calling himself Dr. Gee, used the story to sell fake 'alien' oil-detecting devices. Reporter J.P. Cahn exposed them in True magazine in 1952, and the two were later convicted of fraud.
