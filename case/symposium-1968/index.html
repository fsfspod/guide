The 1968 House UFO Symposium

Catalog FG-041When July 29, 1968Where U.S. Capitol

Scientists, including Carl Sagan, argue about UFOs before Congress.

The House Committee on Science and Astronautics held a one-day symposium with six scientists, among them Hynek, atmospheric physicist James McDonald and astronomer Carl Sagan. Views ranged from urging serious study to firm skepticism.
