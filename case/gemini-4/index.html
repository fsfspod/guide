McDivitt's Gemini 4 Object

Catalog FG-033When June 1965Where Earth orbit, over Hawaii

An astronaut photographs a cylinder with an arm sticking out of it. The photo NASA released showed something else.

On Gemini 4, while Ed White slept, command pilot James McDivitt saw a white, cylindrical object with what looked like a pole or arm sticking out of it. He took a few photographs. After the flight, a frame showing a bright tadpole-shaped streak was released as the object, but it turned out to be sunlight glaring on the window. McDivitt said for the rest of his life that he saw something real, but probably a piece of man-made debris, not a spacecraft from elsewhere.

What's documented

  • McDivitt's own accounts and interviews.
  • NASA's mission photography, including the frame later identified as window glare.

What's claimed

  • A structured alien craft photographed by an astronaut.

Best explanation so far

Space historian James Oberg argues it was most likely the spent Titan II second stage flying nearby, made hard to recognize by glare, dirty windows and viewing angle. McDivitt agreed it was probably man-made.
