The Tehran F-4 Chase

Catalog FG-048When September 19, 1976Where Tehran, Iran

Iranian jets lose instruments as they approach a brilliant light. A U.S. intelligence memo followed.

Residents reported a bright light, and the Iranian Air Force sent two F-4 Phantom jets. Each pilot said instruments or weapons controls failed as he closed in, and one reported a smaller bright object separating from the main light. A Defense Intelligence Agency report described it as a classic case.
