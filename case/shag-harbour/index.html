The Shag Harbour Incident

Catalog FG-039When October 4, 1967Where Shag Harbour, Nova Scotia, Canada

Lights hit the water off a fishing village, and the Canadian Navy came looking.

Witnesses, including police officers, saw a row of lights descend into the sea. Boats rushed out and found a patch of yellow foam but no aircraft and no survivors. Navy divers searched for days and found nothing. Canadian records list the event as unidentified.
