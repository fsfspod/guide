House Armed Services UFO Hearing

Catalog FG-036When April 5, 1966Where U.S. Capitol

Congress's first public look at UFOs.

Prompted by the Michigan sightings, the House Armed Services Committee heard from Air Force Secretary Harold Brown, Hynek and Blue Book head Major Hector Quintanilla. The Air Force agreed to fund an independent university study, which became the Condon Committee.
