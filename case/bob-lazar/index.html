Bob Lazar and S-4

Catalog FG-064When 1989Where Las Vegas, Nevada

A man on TV says he worked on alien craft near Area 51.

In interviews with Las Vegas reporter George Knapp, Bob Lazar said he had worked at a site called S-4 reverse-engineering alien craft powered by 'element 115.' Area 51 entered pop culture almost overnight.

The ledger

What the record shows, what people say, and the best explanation available so far.

What's documented

  • Lazar has given many interviews.
  • Element 115 (moscovium) was created in a lab in 2003; it lasts a fraction of a second.

What's claimed

  • He worked on alien technology.

Best explanation so far

No records support the degrees he says he earned at MIT and Caltech, and the element 115 he described does not match real moscovium. His supporters say records were erased.
