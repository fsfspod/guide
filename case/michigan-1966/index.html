Swamp Gas in Michigan

Catalog FG-035When March 1966Where Dexter and Hillsdale, Michigan

Two words from an astronomer led Congress to its first UFO hearing.

Dozens of people, including college students in a dorm, reported glowing lights over marshes. J. Allen Hynek said at a press conference that marsh gas was a possible cause for some reports. The press turned it into 'swamp gas,' and the public felt mocked. House Minority Leader Gerald Ford, who represented Michigan, called for a congressional inquiry.

Best explanation so far

Hynek later said he only offered marsh gas for a few of the reports. The real legacy was political: the backlash led to the Condon Committee.
