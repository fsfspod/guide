The Disappearance of Frederick Valentich

Catalog FG-052When October 21, 1978Where Bass Strait, Australia

A young pilot radios that something is hovering over him. Then he is gone.

Frederick Valentich, 20, was flying a Cessna 182 to King Island when he told air traffic control that a long object with lights was near his aircraft. His last transmission was followed by metallic noises. Neither he nor the plane was ever found.

Best explanation so far

Investigators considered disorientation, in which a pilot flying inverted can see his own lights reflected, a crash at sea, or a deliberate disappearance. The truth is unknown.
