The STS-48 'Zigzag' Video

Catalog FG-066When September 15, 1991Where Space Shuttle Discovery, in orbit

Shuttle video shows dots drifting, then darting away after a flash. It became a UFO classic.

A camera in Discovery's payload bay recorded several small lights drifting near the Earth's horizon. After a flash, one of the dots appears to change direction sharply and others speed away. The clip spread widely on television in the 1990s as proof of craft maneuvering near the shuttle.

What's claimed

  • Craft dodging a weapon or reacting to the shuttle.

Best explanation so far

James Oberg matched the flash to a steering jet firing as the autopilot corrected a slow drift. The dots are small pieces of debris and ice near the shuttle, lit as it came out of Earth's shadow, and the jet's exhaust pushed them in new directions.
