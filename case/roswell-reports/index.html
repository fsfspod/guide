The Air Force Roswell Reports

Catalog FG-067When 1994 and 1997Where Washington, D.C.

The Air Force reopens Roswell and names Project Mogul.

After a request from Rep. Steven Schiff, the GAO searched for Roswell records, and the Air Force published its own investigation in 1994 identifying a Project Mogul balloon. A second report in 1997, 'The Roswell Report: Case Closed,' addressed the alien body claims.
