The 'Estimate of the Situation'

Catalog FG-011When Late summer 1948Where Project Sign, Wright Field, Ohio

A report said to conclude the saucers were interplanetary, then ordered destroyed.

According to Captain Edward Ruppelt, who later ran Project Blue Book, Project Sign staff wrote a top secret estimate arguing that some saucers were interplanetary craft. Chief of Staff General Hoyt Vandenberg rejected it for lack of proof, and copies were reportedly burned. Project Sign was replaced by the more dismissive Project Grudge in 1949.
