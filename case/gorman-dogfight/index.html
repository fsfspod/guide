The Gorman Dogfight

Catalog FG-012When October 1, 1948Where Fargo, North Dakota

A pilot spent half an hour in a 'dogfight' with a small blinking light.

Lt. George Gorman of the North Dakota Air National Guard chased a small white light over Fargo for about 27 minutes. He said it turned sharply and seemed to make passes at his F-51.

What's claimed

  • An intelligent craft engaged a fighter pilot.

Best explanation so far

Investigators concluded he was chasing a lighted weather balloon. A balloon's apparent motion can look dramatic from a turning plane.
