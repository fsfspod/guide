The Incident at Exeter

Catalog FG-031When September 3, 1965Where Exeter, New Hampshire

A teenager and two police officers watch pulsing red lights over a field.

Norman Muscarello ran to the police station after a large object with pulsing red lights swooped toward him. Officers Eugene Bertrand and David Hunt returned with him and saw the lights themselves. John Fuller's book 'Incident at Exeter' brought the case national attention.

Best explanation so far

The Air Force first said it was a military refueling exercise, then changed the case to unidentified. Some researchers think the lights matched a refueling aircraft.
