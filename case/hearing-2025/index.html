The 2025 Task Force Hearing & the Yemen Video

Catalog FG-099When September 9, 2025Where U.S. Capitol

Congress is shown a drone video of a Hellfire missile hitting an orb that keeps going.

Rep. Anna Paulina Luna's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets held a hearing on UAP transparency and whistleblower protection. Witnesses included journalist George Knapp and veterans Jeffrey Nuccetelli, Dylan Borland and Alexandro Wiggins. Rep. Eric Burlison showed footage from off Yemen in which an MQ-9 drone's Hellfire missile strikes a glowing orb, which appears to keep moving.
