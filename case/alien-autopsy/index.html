Hoax

The Alien Autopsy Film

Catalog FG-069When 1995Where London, England

Black-and-white footage of an autopsy on a Roswell alien, broadcast around the world.

Promoter Ray Santilli released film he said a military cameraman shot in 1947. It aired worldwide. In 2006 Santilli admitted it was a recreation staged with a dummy made by a sculptor, though he claimed it was based on real footage he had once seen.
