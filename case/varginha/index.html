The Varginha Creature

Catalog FG-070When January 20, 1996Where Varginha, Brazil

Three young women report a strange creature crouched by a wall. Rumors of captures follow.

Three young women said they saw a creature with brown skin, large red eyes and bumps on its head. Rumors spread of military teams capturing creatures and taking them to a hospital. Brazil's 'Roswell' was born.
