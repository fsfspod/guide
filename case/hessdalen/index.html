The Hessdalen Lights

Catalog FG-058When 1981 – presentWhere Hessdalen Valley, Norway

A Norwegian valley where strange lights are studied with permanent instruments.

From late 1981, residents of a small valley reported lights hovering and drifting, sometimes several times a week. Project Hessdalen began in 1983 with cameras, radar and magnetometers, and an automatic monitoring station has run since 1998.

Best explanation so far

Scientists suspect a natural phenomenon, possibly glowing plasma tied to the valley's geology, mixed with car lights and aircraft. It is a rare example of a UAP studied with ongoing instruments.
