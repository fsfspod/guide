Hoax

Gulf Breeze

Catalog FG-063When 1987–1990Where Gulf Breeze, Florida

Dramatic Polaroids of a glowing craft, and a model found in the attic.

Ed Walters published Polaroid photos of a craft over Gulf Breeze, and the town became a UFO destination. In 1990 the new owners of his former home found a model resembling the craft hidden in the attic.
