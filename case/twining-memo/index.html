The Twining Memo

Catalog FG-008When September 23, 1947Where Air Materiel Command, Wright Field, Ohio

A top general tells the Pentagon the saucer reports deserve serious study.

Lt. General Nathan Twining, head of Air Materiel Command, wrote that the reported phenomenon was something real and not imaginary, and recommended a formal investigation. The memo led directly to Project Sign in early 1948, the first official U.S. UFO study.

What's documented

  • The memo is genuine and widely published.

What's claimed

  • It proves the military knew the objects were alien craft.

Best explanation so far

The memo shows the military took the reports seriously, mostly out of fear of Soviet technology. It makes no claim about origin and notes there was no physical evidence such as crash debris.
