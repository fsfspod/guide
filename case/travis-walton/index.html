Travis Walton

Catalog FG-047When November 5, 1975Where Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, Arizona

A logger vanishes for five days after his crew sees him struck by a beam.

Six coworkers said Travis Walton approached a glowing craft and was knocked down by a beam, and they fled. He reappeared five days later saying he had woken on a craft with beings. The story became the 1993 film 'Fire in the Sky.'

What's documented

  • A five-day missing-person search took place.
  • Polygraph results were mixed; crew members passed some tests.

What's claimed

  • A genuine abduction with many witnesses.

Best explanation so far

Critics argue the crew had a motive to break a logging contract they were behind on. Walton and the crew have stood by the story for decades.
