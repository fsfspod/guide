Lonnie Zamora at Socorro

Catalog FG-030When April 24, 1964Where Socorro, New Mexico

A respected police officer sees a landed egg-shaped craft and two small figures.

Officer Lonnie Zamora was chasing a speeder when he heard a roar and saw a flame. He found a white, egg-shaped object on legs in a gully, with two small figures in white beside it. The object rose with a roar and flew off. Investigators found scorch marks and imprints. Hynek looked into it personally.

What's documented

  • Physical traces were photographed and measured.
  • Blue Book listed it as unidentified, one of its best-known unknowns.

What's claimed

  • A landed alien craft with occupants.

Best explanation so far

A theory that it was a student prank at New Mexico Tech has circulated for decades, along with suggestions of a secret test vehicle. Neither has been proven.
