The Hudson Valley Boomerang

Catalog FG-059When 1982–1986Where Hudson Valley, New York

Thousands saw a huge, silent boomerang of lights. A group of pilots had a secret.

For several years, thousands of people in New York and Connecticut reported a giant V or boomerang of lights moving slowly overhead. Some said it blocked out the stars.

Best explanation so far

Investigators found a group of private pilots flying light planes in tight formation at night, sometimes turning their lights off and on. Not every report fits, but most do.
