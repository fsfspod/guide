The Mexican Air Force Infrared Video

Catalog FG-077When March 5, 2004Where Over Campeche, Mexico

A drug-surveillance plane films eleven glowing objects on infrared. They didn't move.

A Mexican Air Force surveillance aircraft on an anti-drug patrol recorded a group of bright lights on its infrared camera over the state of Campeche. The crew could not see them with the naked eye, and radar briefly showed targets. Mexico's Defense Ministry allowed the video to be released in May 2004, and it made international headlines as an official UFO video.

Best explanation so far

Researchers matched the lights to gas flares burning on oil platforms in the Cantarell offshore field, far over the horizon. Infrared cameras pick up their heat even when the eye can't see them. The 'objects' kept a fixed pattern because they were fixed.
