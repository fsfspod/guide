On the record

Projects Sign, Grudge & Blue Book

Catalog FG-017When 1948–1969Where Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio

Twenty-two years of Air Force UFO investigation: 12,618 reports, 701 unidentified.

Project Sign (1948) became Project Grudge (1949) and then Project Blue Book (1952). Under Captain Edward Ruppelt, who coined the term 'unidentified flying object,' Blue Book took reports seriously. Later it was understaffed and often criticized for forcing explanations onto cases. Astronomer J. Allen Hynek served as its scientific consultant for most of its life and grew more convinced over time that some cases deserved real study. The Air Force closed Blue Book on December 17, 1969.

What's claimed

Best explanation so far

This is the largest public archive of UFO reports in existence. Most cases have ordinary explanations, though the quality of the investigations varies a lot.
