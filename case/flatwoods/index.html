The Flatwoods Monster

Catalog FG-021When September 12, 1952Where Flatwoods, West Virginia

A group of kids and adults meet a hissing, glowing figure on a dark hillside.

After seeing a bright light streak across the sky and land on a hill, a group of boys and local mother Kathleen May went to look. Their flashlight caught a tall figure with a spade-shaped head and glowing eyes, and an awful smell. Several of them became ill afterward.

What's documented

  • Multiple witnesses gave consistent accounts to reporters.
  • A bright meteor was seen over several states that night.

What's claimed

  • An alien landed in rural West Virginia.

Best explanation so far

Investigator Joe Nickell proposed a mix of the meteor, a pulsing aircraft beacon and a startled barn owl in a tree, magnified by fear in the dark.
