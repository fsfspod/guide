The McMinnville Photos

Catalog FG-015When May 11, 1950Where Near McMinnville, Oregon

Two farm photos of a disc that have been argued over for 75 years.

Paul Trent took two photographs of a metallic disc over his farm while his wife Evelyn watched. The photos ran in Life magazine. The Condon Committee's photo analyst wrote that the case was one of the few in which every factor seemed consistent with a real, unusual flying object.
