Uruguay's Air Force UFO Commission

Catalog FG-054When 1979 – presentWhere Montevideo, Uruguay

One of the longest-running official UFO investigation units in the world.

Uruguay's Air Force set up the Comisión Receptora e Investigadora de Denuncias de Objetos Voladores No Identificados (CRIDOVNI) in 1979 to collect and investigate reports. It continues to take public reports and issue findings, and most of its cases are resolved as aircraft, satellites, planets and other ordinary objects.
