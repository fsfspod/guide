The Navy Videos Go Official

Catalog FG-087When April 27, 2020Where The Pentagon

The Pentagon formally releases FLIR1, Gimbal and GoFast.

The Department of Defense formally released the three Navy videos already circulating, saying it wanted to clear up confusion about whether they were real. In August 2020 the Navy's Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force was created.
