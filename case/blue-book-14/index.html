Blue Book Special Report No. 14

Catalog FG-023When 1955Where Battelle Memorial Institute, Ohio

The first big statistical study of UFO reports, and an argument still running.

Under Air Force contract, Battelle analyzed about 3,200 reports from 1947 to 1952. It found that the better the quality of a report, the more likely it was to remain 'unknown,' with roughly a fifth of all cases unexplained. Yet the report concluded there was no evidence of craft beyond known technology.
