Brazil's Official UFO Night

Catalog FG-062When May 19, 1986Where São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Minas Gerais and Goiás, Brazil

Radar tracks 21 objects, five fighter jets scramble, and the Air Minister says he has no explanation.

On the night of May 19, civilian and military witnesses across southeastern Brazil reported lights, and the Air Force's air defense radar tracked targets. Five fighter jets were sent to intercept. Some pilots saw lights the radar didn't register, and the Air Force counted only sightings confirmed both ways. Four days later, Air Minister Brigadier Octávio Moreira Lima told reporters, 'Technically, I would tell you that we have no explanation.'

Weigh the evidence

The ledger

What the record shows, what people say, and the best explanation available so far.

What's documented

  • The minister's televised press conference.
  • An Air Force report dated June 2, 1986, released in 2009. It described the objects as solid and showing a degree of intelligence.
  • Most of the Air Force's UFO files were later moved to Brazil's National Archives.

What's claimed

  • Brazil's military chased alien craft for hours.

Best explanation so far

No full explanation has been published. Skeptics point to radar effects, bright stars and planets, and the difficulty of pilots judging distant lights at night.
