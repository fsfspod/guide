The Majestic 12 Documents

Catalog FG-060When 1984–1987Where Mailed to a TV producer

Supposed top secret papers about a secret UFO control group. The FBI called them bogus.

In 1984, producer Jaime Shandera received a roll of film showing a briefing paper for President-elect Eisenhower describing 'Majestic 12,' a secret group managing the Roswell recovery. The documents were made public in 1987 and inspired countless sequels.
