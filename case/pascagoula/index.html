The Pascagoula Abduction

Catalog FG-045When October 11, 1973Where Pascagoula, Mississippi

Two shipyard workers say they were floated into a craft while fishing.

Charles Hickson and Calvin Parker told the sheriff that gray, wrinkled beings with claw-like hands carried them into a craft. Officers left them alone in a room with a hidden recorder, and the men sounded genuinely frightened.
