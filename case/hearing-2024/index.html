'Exposing the Truth' Hearing

Catalog FG-096When November 13, 2024Where U.S. Capitol

Luis Elizondo and others testify about secrecy and UAP.

House Oversight subcommittees heard from Luis Elizondo, retired Navy Rear Admiral Tim Gallaudet, former NASA official Michael Gold and journalist Michael Shellenberger. Witnesses argued that secret programs exist and urged more transparency.
