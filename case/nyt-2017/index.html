The New York Times Story

Catalog FG-085When December 16, 2017Where New York, New York

A front-page story reveals a secret Pentagon program and two Navy videos.

Reporters Helene Cooper, Ralph Blumenthal and Leslie Kean revealed the Pentagon's AATIP program and published the Nimitz and Gimbal videos. The same week, To the Stars Academy, co-founded by musician Tom DeLonge, released them too. Luis Elizondo became the public face of the story.
