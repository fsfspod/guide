The Tremonton Film

Catalog FG-020When July 2, 1952Where Near Tremonton, Utah

A Navy photographer's 16mm film of shining dots, reviewed by the CIA. It reappeared in 2026.

Navy Chief Photographer Delbert Newhouse filmed a group of bright objects milling in the sky from beside a highway. Navy photo analysts said they could not identify the objects. The film was shown to the CIA-sponsored Robertson Panel in January 1953. A copy was included in the sixth PURSUE release in September 2026.

Best explanation so far

The Robertson Panel concluded the objects were most likely birds such as gulls reflecting sunlight. Not everyone has accepted that.
