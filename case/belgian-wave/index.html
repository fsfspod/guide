The Belgian Wave

Catalog FG-065When November 1989 – 1990Where Belgium

Triangles over Belgium, F-16s scrambled, and a photo that turned out to be fake.

Thousands of Belgians reported large triangular craft with lights at each corner. On the night of March 30–31, 1990, the Belgian Air Force scrambled F-16s after radar contacts, and the military openly cooperated with civilian researchers.

Best explanation so far

The famous Petit-Rechain triangle photo was admitted to be a styrofoam model in 2011. The F-16 radar locks were likely weather effects. Many ground sightings remain unexplained.
