The Chiles–Whitted Encounter

Catalog FG-010When July 24, 1948Where Near Montgomery, Alabama

Two airline pilots describe a glowing, windowed cigar rushing past their DC-3.

Eastern Air Lines pilots Clarence Chiles and John Whitted said a wingless, cigar-shaped object with two rows of lit windows shot past their plane before dawn. A passenger saw a streak of light. The case helped push Project Sign's staff to draft their 'Estimate of the Situation.'

What's claimed

Best explanation so far

Astronomer J. Allen Hynek and others concluded it was a bright fireball. The 'windows' fit the glowing, fragmenting trail of a large meteor.
