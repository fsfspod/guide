On the record

AAWSAP and AATIP

Catalog FG-081When 2007–2012Where Defense Intelligence Agency, Washington, D.C.

A small Pentagon study, pushed by Senator Harry Reid, that nobody knew about.

With support from Senate Majority Leader Harry Reid, the Defense Intelligence Agency awarded about $22 million to Robert Bigelow's company to study advanced aerospace threats, a program called AAWSAP. The name AATIP is often used for the same effort. It produced technical papers on topics like warp drives and wormholes, and investigated some UFO reports. Official funding ended around 2012.
