PURSUE: The 2026 File Releases

Catalog FG-100When February – September 2026Where Department of War, war.gov/UFO

A presidential directive sends hundreds of UFO files into public view in six releases.

In February 2026 President Trump directed agencies to identify and declassify UFO and UAP files. The Department of War launched PURSUE (the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters) on May 8, 2026, with more than 150 files. Five more releases followed through September 18, 2026, drawing on the Pentagon, CIA, FBI, NASA, the Department of Energy, the State Department and others. Highlights include Apollo-era photos and transcripts, green orb reports near a military facility, an infrared video of objects near Iran, and a copy of the 1952 Tremonton film.

Best explanation so far

Officials said the files do not confirm extraterrestrial life and invited the public to draw its own conclusions. Skeptics noted much of the material was already public or showed ordinary effects. Many files are raw and unanalyzed, which makes them good practice for a friendly skeptic.
