GEIPAN: France's Official UAP Office

Catalog FG-049When 1977 – presentWhere CNES, Toulouse, France

The longest-running government UAP office in the world publishes its cases online.

France's space agency CNES created GEPAN in 1977, later renamed SEPRA and then GEIPAN. It investigates reports and posts case files online with a classification: identified, probably identified, insufficient data, or unidentified after investigation.
