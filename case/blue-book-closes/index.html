Blue Book Closes

Catalog FG-042When December 17, 1969Where Washington, D.C.

The Air Force gets out of the UFO business.

Air Force Secretary Robert Seamans announced the end of Project Blue Book, citing the Condon report. The Air Force said no UFO had threatened national security or shown technology beyond present knowledge, and there was no evidence they were extraterrestrial.
