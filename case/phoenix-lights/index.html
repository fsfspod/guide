The Phoenix Lights

Catalog FG-071When March 13, 1997Where Phoenix, Arizona

Two separate events the same night, often merged into one mystery.

Around 8 p.m., people across Arizona saw a V-shaped arrangement of lights move slowly across the state. Around 10 p.m., a row of bright stationary lights appeared south of Phoenix and was filmed by many. Governor Fife Symington held a press conference with an aide in an alien costume, then said years later he had seen the lights himself.

The ledger

What the record shows, what people say, and the best explanation available so far.

What's documented

  • Many videos of the 10 p.m. lights.
  • The Air National Guard confirmed dropping flares that night.

What's claimed

  • A mile-wide craft passed over the city.

Best explanation so far

The 10 p.m. lights match LUU-2 flares dropped by A-10 jets over the Barry Goldwater range. The 8 p.m. V is widely thought to be planes flying in formation, though some witnesses insist it was one solid object.
