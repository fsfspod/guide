The O'Hare Airport Sighting

Catalog FG-080When November 7, 2006Where Chicago O'Hare International Airport

Airline workers watch a dark disc hover over a gate, then shoot up through the clouds.

United Airlines employees and pilots reported a gray, disc-shaped object hovering over Gate C17. Some said it shot upward and left a hole in the clouds. The Chicago Tribune broke the story in early 2007.
