The Disclosure Project Press Conference

Catalog FG-074When May 9, 2001Where National Press Club, Washington, D.C.

Twenty witnesses call for hearings about secret UFO knowledge.

Organized by Steven Greer, former military, government and aviation personnel described UFO encounters and claimed secret programs. They asked Congress to hold hearings.
