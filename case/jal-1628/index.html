Japan Air Lines Flight 1628

Catalog FG-061When November 17, 1986Where Over Alaska

A 747 cargo crew says a huge object followed them across Alaska.

Captain Kenju Terauchi reported large lights pacing his Boeing 747 cargo flight, including an object he said was far bigger than an aircraft carrier. Ground radar seemed to show returns at times. The FAA investigated and released its files.
