Trans-en-Provence

Catalog FG-057When January 8, 1981Where Trans-en-Provence, France

The French government's scientists analyze a landing trace in a garden.

Farmer Renato Nicolaï said a lead-colored, saucer-shaped craft landed briefly on his property and left. GEPAN, France's official UAP unit at its space agency, collected soil and plant samples and reported physical changes it could not explain.

Best explanation so far

Critics argue the trace could have come from a vehicle and the lab work was not as rigorous as presented. It is still one of the most scientifically studied trace cases.
