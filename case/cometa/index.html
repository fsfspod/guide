The COMETA Report

Catalog FG-073When 1999Where France

Retired French generals and experts publish a report taking UAP seriously.

A private group of retired French military officers and experts, many connected to France's national defense institute, published 'UFOs and Defense.' It argued that extraterrestrial explanations were the most likely for some cases and urged international study.
