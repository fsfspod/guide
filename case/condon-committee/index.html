On the record

The Condon Committee

Catalog FG-040When 1966–1969Where University of Colorado, Boulder

An independent scientific study concludes UFOs are not worth further study. Its own data was messier.

The Air Force paid the University of Colorado to study UFOs under physicist Edward Condon. His summary, published in January 1969, said nothing of scientific value had come from UFO study in 21 years. A National Academy of Sciences panel endorsed it. Critics point out that a good share of the cases the team studied stayed unexplained, and a leaked internal memo seemed to show the study was designed to reach a negative conclusion.

Best explanation so far

The report gave the Air Force its reason to close Blue Book. It remains the most detailed scientific UFO study of its era, and both skeptics and believers quote it.
