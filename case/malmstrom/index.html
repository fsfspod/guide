Malmstrom Missile Shutdown

Catalog FG-038When March 16, 1967Where Malmstrom Air Force Base, Montana

Ten nuclear missiles went offline. Later witnesses said a UFO was overhead.

All ten Minuteman missiles in Echo Flight suddenly went into a no-go state. The Air Force investigated and suspected an electrical pulse in the system. Decades later, former officer Robert Salas and others said guards reported a glowing red object over the site, and that a similar shutdown hit another flight.

Best explanation so far

Records from the time describe the shutdown but do not mention UFOs. The UFO link rests on recollections that surfaced years later and differ in their details.
