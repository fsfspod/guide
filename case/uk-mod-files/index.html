Britain's UFO Files & Project Condign

Catalog FG-079When 2000–2013Where United Kingdom

A secret UK intelligence study and decades of Ministry of Defence files go public.

The UK Defence Intelligence Staff completed a study called Project Condign in 2000. Released in 2006, it found no evidence of intelligent craft and attributed many reports to natural atmospheric effects such as buoyant plasma. The Ministry of Defence closed its UFO desk in 2009 and transferred its files to the National Archives in batches through 2013.
