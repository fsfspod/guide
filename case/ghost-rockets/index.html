Ghost Rockets of Scandinavia

Catalog FG-004When Summer 1946Where Sweden and Finland

Thousands of reports of rocket-like objects streaking over Sweden.

In 1946 Swedish civilians reported around 2,000 rocket- or cigar-shaped objects, some said to crash into lakes. With German V-weapons fresh in memory, the Swedish military suspected Soviet tests using captured technology and formed an investigating committee. Divers searched Lake Kölmjärv after a reported crash and found nothing.

What's documented

What's claimed

  • Soviet missile tests.
  • Craft from elsewhere.

Best explanation so far

Investigators concluded most reports were meteors (a meteor shower peaked that August) and misidentified ordinary objects. A small remainder stayed unexplained.
