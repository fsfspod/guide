The Gemini 7 'Bogey'

Catalog FG-034When December 1965Where Earth orbit

Frank Borman reports a 'bogey' and hundreds of little particles. The transcript reappeared in the 2026 file release.

During the two-week Gemini 7 mission, Frank Borman radioed that he had a 'bogey' in sight, along with a field of hundreds of small particles. The word comes from military pilot slang for an unidentified aircraft, and UFO writers later held it up as an astronaut sighting. The transcript was included in the first PURSUE release in May 2026.

Best explanation so far

James Oberg's analysis of the orbits points to 'a brighter-than-average piece of booster-associated debris,' most likely the rocket stage that launched them. Borman himself later said it was not a UFO.
