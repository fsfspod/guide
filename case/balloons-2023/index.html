The 2023 Balloon Shootdowns

Catalog FG-091When February 4–12, 2023Where South Carolina, Alaska, Yukon, Lake Huron

A Chinese spy balloon is shot down, followed by three more mystery objects in a week.

After shooting down a Chinese surveillance balloon off South Carolina on February 4, the U.S. adjusted its radar filters and spotted more slow objects. Fighters shot down objects over Alaska, Canada's Yukon and Lake Huron. Officials said the last three were most likely harmless commercial or hobby balloons; an Illinois club reported losing one of its balloons around that time.
