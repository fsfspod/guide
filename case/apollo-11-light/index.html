Apollo 11's Traveling Light

Catalog FG-043When July 1969Where On the way to the Moon

Buzz Aldrin sees a light pacing the spacecraft. A science fiction writer's hoax made the story much bigger.

On the way to the Moon, the Apollo 11 crew saw a light out the window that seemed to travel alongside them. They asked Mission Control where their discarded rocket stage was and were told it was thousands of miles away. Aldrin talked about the sighting in later interviews and said his words were often taken out of context. Separately, a fabricated story that Neil Armstrong saw alien craft on the lunar surface spread widely; it has been traced to a hoax by science fiction writer Otto Binder.

What's documented

What's claimed

  • Alien craft followed Apollo 11 to the Moon.
  • Armstrong reported aliens watching from a crater.

Best explanation so far

The most likely candidates are panels that separated from the rocket when the spacecraft pulled away, which would have traveled along a similar path. Aldrin has said it was probably one of them. The Armstrong quote is a hoax.
