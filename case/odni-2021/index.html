The ODNI Preliminary Assessment

Catalog FG-088When June 25, 2021Where Office of the Director of National Intelligence

The first official intelligence report on UAP: 144 cases, 1 explained.

Required by Congress, the report reviewed 144 military reports from 2004 to 2021. One was identified as a large deflating balloon. Eighteen reports described unusual movement patterns. The report sorted possible explanations into airborne clutter, natural atmospheric effects, secret U.S. programs, foreign technology, and 'other.'
