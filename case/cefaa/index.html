Chile's CEFAA and the Navy Helicopter Video

Catalog FG-083When 1997 – present (video: November 11, 2014)Where Chile

Chile's official UFO committee releases a Navy video it calls unexplained. Online analysts found the airliner.

Chile's civil aviation authority runs the Committee for the Study of Anomalous Aerial Phenomena (CEFAA), which has investigated reports since the late 1990s. In January 2017 it released infrared and color video taken from a Chilean Navy helicopter in November 2014, showing an object leaving a dark trail over the ocean. After two years of study, the committee said it could not identify the object.

Best explanation so far

Within weeks, analysts at Metabunk matched the timing and direction to Iberia flight IB6830, an Airbus A340 climbing out of Santiago. The bright 'object' is the engines' heat in infrared, and the trail is a contrail. It's a lesson in how an official 'unidentified' can mean 'not yet identified.'
