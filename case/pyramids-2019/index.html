The 2019 'Pyramids' & Omaha Sphere

Catalog FG-086When July 2019Where Off Southern California

Leaked Navy footage of triangle-shaped lights and a sphere that seemed to enter the sea.

In 2021 filmmaker Jeremy Corbell leaked footage from Navy ships, including blinking triangles filmed through night-vision gear from the USS Russell and a sphere filmed from the USS Omaha that seemed to drop into the ocean. The Pentagon confirmed the footage was taken by Navy personnel.

Best explanation so far

The triangle shape matches the aperture of the night-vision camera, which turns any point of light into a triangle. The ships were also swarmed by drones in the same period. The sphere has been suggested to be a balloon or drone.
