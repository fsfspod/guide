The Stephenville Lights

Catalog FG-082When January 8, 2008Where Stephenville, Texas

A small Texas town sees a huge set of lights, and the Air Force changes its story.

Dozens of residents, including a pilot and a police officer, reported large, fast-moving lights. The Air Force first said no aircraft were in the area, then said ten F-16s had been training nearby. A civilian analysis of FAA radar data suggested an unidentified target.
